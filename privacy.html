Privacy policy

This OAuth app is used only by its owner, with rclone running on his own computers, to read and write files in his own Google Drive.

  • No data is collected, stored or shared by this app or by anyone operating it. Files travel directly between Google Drive and the owner's computer.
  • The access token stays on the owner's computer, in rclone's configuration file.
  • There are no other users, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
